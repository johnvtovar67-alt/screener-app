## Research use versus storage and publication

[FMP's historical-data FAQ](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/faqs), under “How can I get access to historical financial data through your API?”, identifies historical endpoints as inputs for analysis and research.

[FMP's September 17, 2026 publication guidance](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/insights/platform/can-you-publish-fmpsourced-charts-tables-or-research-in-a-newsletter) supports an individual's private, non-business notebook or spreadsheet. It separately says that calculated returns, rankings, signals and reports can require display rights; transformation and a small number of values do not create an exemption. Ultimate access alone does not grant public display or redistribution rights.

[FMP Terms of Service](https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/terms-of-service), page marked last updated August 1, 2023:

- Sections 2.1–2.2 make access limited to the subscribed purpose and period; 2.2.1 limits an individual license to personal, non-business use and requires prior written approval for copying/downloading content.
- Sections 2.2, 2.2.2 and 2.6.1 restrict publishing/displaying/transferring source data and information derived from the service. No signed display or redistribution exception was found in the searched repository or relevant subscription correspondence.
- Section 2.8 requires controlled storage/processing locations and notification of their IP/domain aliases. Existing location-notification evidence was not verified.
- Sections 6.2–6.3 require deletion on termination, including cached data. Section 5 has conditional retention language; it is insufficient to infer unrestricted permanent raw-data retention.
- Section 2.6.2 references an Acceptable Data Use Policy. The linked URL could not be fetched; its `site.` variant returned 404. No broader rights were inferred.

## Execution decision

Proceed only as the account holder's **private, bounded personal research using ephemeral in-memory licensed inputs**. Do not save response bodies, normalized prices, membership rows, source-price tables, screenshots, or reconstructable price paths. Do not send raw licensed inputs into model context. This choice does not claim that permanent retention has been approved.

Deliver only the private aggregate numerical research summary to the account holder. The public GitHub checkpoint may preserve nonproprietary research code, frozen formulas, chosen symbol/date parameters, endpoint names, methodology, non-price acquisition diagnostics, limitations and this permissions note. **Do not publish market-derived numerical findings, rankings, price paths or signal event tables** without an applicable written display agreement. The private owner report must not be committed to the public repository.

Expanding into retained licensed datasets, public numerical reports, multi-user applications or business research is **NO-GO** until the applicable written retention/display/commercial permissions and remaining subscription budget are evidenced. A successful price response cannot settle those questions.
