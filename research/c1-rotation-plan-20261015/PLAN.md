## Sale, replacement and the minimum daily-data clock

Replacement means the first candidate in the source's then-valid ordered queue that survives every entry, held-name, issuer/sector, cash, gap, sizing and risk gate. It is not the next displayed name and never the best future return. Log each rejected candidate. Queued mandatory exits, removals and portfolio drawdown processing retain priority. If the selected position's opening price already breaches its standing stop, record the normal stop/no extra intervention. Do not inspect that day's later low to decide whether an opening intervention is eligible, and do not sell the same shares twice.

For the smallest daily-data comparison, freeze this conservative intervention clock: close t makes the exit decision; execute all normal t+1 opening mandatory sales/risk processing and queued buy attempts first. Only then, before subsequent intraday stop processing/marks/closing decisions, apply the additional B sale at the modeled t+1 opening price. Thus neither its cash nor its freed slot is available to any normal t+1 queued buy. Post its net proceeds once and perform no intraday replacement replan. The next normal t+1 completed-close C1 replan can select a replacement for t+2 open, with the then-valid queue and execution checks. Normal orders using preexisting resources remain source-compatible. Test this ordering explicitly: merely freezing the queue, or reserving proceeds while freeing the slot, is insufficient because the native queue already contains alternatives. This tests delayed daily-clock capital rotation; it does not claim an immediate same-auction purchase or silently force a buy.

The normal simulator can sell and buy at the same modeled opening. The actual-account cash overlay expressly excludes unrecorded sale proceeds (`lib/c1ExecutionCashAuthority.js:6–8,36`). An immediate same-session brokerage comparison therefore requires verified sequential fills, executable quotes and buying power, which daily FMP history does not establish. Do not invent those facts or change the current cash authority. 12 bps applies to each filled leg; do not charge embedded slippage twice. If a broker's proceeds are not yet available, cash remains unavailable until its verified release.
