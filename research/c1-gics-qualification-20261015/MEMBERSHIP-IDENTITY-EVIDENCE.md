# Membership and security identity: bounded primary-source qualification

Scope: December 31, 2024 through April 3, 2025. This review made zero FMP
requests, acquired no prices, and used no original frozen archives. GICS is an
explicitly modified research input for both arms. The 1,750-request acquisition
remains unauthorized.

**Result: the regular-member candidate and share-class joins are well supported;
complete daily index-action coverage, including two spin-offs, is not certified.**
Do not convert a 503-line endpoint reconciliation into a claim that there were
503 eligible index lines on every intervening session.

## Confirmed facts and conditional counts

The existing private reconciliation joins every one of the 503 December 31 SPY
SEC common-equity name/class lines to the dated public snapshot: 456 canonical
name matches, 47 explicitly reviewed aliases, zero unresolved. There are 500
issuer CIKs. Alphabet A/C, Fox A/B and News Corp A/B remain separate securities.
Issuer CIK is a grouping key, never the unique security key. Berkshire B and
Brown-Forman B punctuation aliases do not change their share classes.

[S&P's March 7 release](https://press.spglobal.com/2025-03-07-DoorDash%2C-TKO-Group-Holdings%2C-Williams-Sonoma-and-Expand-Energy-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Others-to-Join-S-P-100%2C-S-P-MidCap-400-and-S-P-SmallCap-600)
confirms four additions and four removals before the March 24 open: DASH, EXE,
TKO and WSM enter; BWA, CE, FMC and TFX leave. Its table supplies contemporaneous
GICS sectors. The announcement date belongs in `known_on`; March 24 belongs in
`valid_from`/`valid_to`. Effective-date membership prevents pre-announcement or
pre-effective eligibility. Use a conservative next-session availability bound
if the release's exact publication timestamp has not been independently pinned.

Applying only these actions produces 503 regular lines and a union of 507
security lines / 504 issuer CIKs, matching the dated March 26 public snapshot.
This is a **conditional regular-member count**, not the complete daily roster.
The press headline archive does not establish the absence of temporary index
corporate actions. ETF holdings are corroboration rather than authoritative
index membership; a distribution entitlement can appear before regular trading.

as proof of either 2025 event's implementation.

If MRP was a 500 line only February 7 and SNDK only February 24, the union would
be 509, with 504 rather than 503 lines on those two sessions. Those are a
scenario to check, **not verified reconstruction results**. They would not have
sufficient stand-alone C1 momentum history and must fail the unchanged warmup
rule; when-issued observations must not be treated as a long price history.
That does not excuse ignoring their membership or mandatory distributions to
any LEN/WDC holdings. Both portfolio arms must receive equivalent entitlements
under their actual pre-event positions and process them using the same frozen
accounting rules. A split-adjusted or total-return price series must not double
count a separately booked distribution.

An additional primary caution comes from [BlackRock's January 31 interim report](https://www.blackrock.com/sa/professional/en/literature/interim-report/ishares-vii-plc-interim-report-en-31-jan-25.pdf):

retrospective evidence of a fund position/entitlement, not proof that MRP was a
tradable index constituent on January 31 or a contemporaneously available GICS
classification. Do not substitute the report's industry heading for GICS.

is readable and contains the XML. No broad connectivity or credentials work is
needed. Extract only identity fields, discard holdings valuations, and preserve
the source hash privately. A corresponding March 31 report is retrospective
corroboration, never an input available on March 24.

## Exact remaining remedies

1. Obtain **one closed-scope S&P 500 action ledger or written index-provider
   confirmation** covering December 31 through April 3: all additions/deletions,
   temporary zero-price spin-offs/placeholders, symbol/class changes, effective
   open/close convention and original announcement availability. Explicitly ask
   about LEN/MRP February 7–10 and WDC/SNDK February 24–25. Confirm there are no
   other actions. A pre-existing provider/fund corporate-action extract can also
   resolve the cases if it identifies the index and effective legs. An absence
   of search results or unchanged endpoint sets is insufficient.
2. For those two events, supply temporary security/class IDs and any effective
   sector interval. Treat mandatory received shares separately from new-entry
   qualification. Do not invent a current GICS label for an earlier placeholder.
3. Use the existing 503 dated name/class + issuer-CIK joins and the four entrant
   filings as explicit research security-line IDs, preserving same-issuer share
   classes. The unresolved action ledger must establish whether any within-window
   ticker/class transitions require a new identity interval. CUSIP/ISIN extraction
   is optional strengthening unless a particular line is ambiguous or reorganized;
   do not turn a complete identifiers master into an unnecessary new gate. Do not
   require hundreds of current FMP profiles to restate the existing reconciliation.

The first remedy is a finite evidence request, not a new data subscription,
infrastructure project or authorization for bulk acquisition. Until the
closed-scope action record is supplied, mark membership continuity unresolved
and keep acquisition closed. Sector continuity requires its own dated evidence
and is covered in the accompanying qualification artifact.
