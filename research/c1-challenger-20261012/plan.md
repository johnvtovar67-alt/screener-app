## Observable regime definitions

All labels for a day's return and new exposure use SPY observations through the PREVIOUS completed session. Fix thresholds before runs:

- Correction: prior-close drawdown from trailing 252-session closing high <=-10%.
- High volatility: annualized sample standard deviation of prior 20 daily returns >=25%.
- Low volatility: same <=15%.
- Strong trend: prior close above prior SMA200 AND trailing63-session return >=10%.
- Recovery: prior close above SMA50, positive 63-session return, and drawdown <=-5%.
- Sideways: absolute 63-session return <5%.

Flags overlap; report overlap counts and never sum them as disjoint attribution. Report per-regime compounded returns, daily Sharpe/Sortino where estimable, worst local drawdown, benchmark excess, turnover, capital utilization, days, contiguous episodes and trade counts. Also calendar years, correction/high-volatility episode tables, and rolling differences. Small regimes and overlapping episodes provide limited evidence. Scaling-state restoration counters are separate from explanatory labels.
