# V16 / Research R6 attention-shock continuation contract

## Freeze and authority

This research-only thesis was specified and locally committed before R4's
result was observed. A separately frozen industry-leadership thesis became
R5/V15 first, so this unchanged design is numbered R6/V16. Neither R4 nor R5
selected or altered these parameters. R6 cannot change production
recommendations. V11 remains the live policy unless a challenger passes every
historical, statistical, strict-placebo, prospective, and
independent-replication gate.

Every session through 2026-09-01 was observable before this freeze. Those
sessions may falsify R6 but cannot establish future alpha.

## Economically distinct hypothesis

R1 through R3 continuously ranked broad momentum or residual-leadership
characteristics, while R4 tested short-term reversal and R5 tests sector-first
industry leadership. R6 instead treats an
unusual burst of trading activity as a discrete attention event. It tests
whether liquid index members already close to a trailing 52-week high, with
positive but bounded five-session follow-through, continue outperforming for
approximately one month.

An entry must have all four causal close-known observations and satisfy:

- current session volume at least 1.5 times its prior 20-session average;
- five-session return above 0% and no more than 12%;
- close no more than 5% below the trailing 252-session high; and
- an observed 20-session excess return versus SPY.

The single primary rank is frozen as:

- 40% logarithmic volume shock;
- 25% proximity to the trailing 252-session high;
- 20% bounded five-session follow-through; and
- 15% 20-session excess return versus SPY.

The portfolio holds up to ten approximately 9.5% positions, evaluates new
events daily, requires a 20-session minimum hold, and uses a 63-session hard
time limit. It caps a sector at three positions/30%, fills at the next open,
rejects opening gaps above 3%, applies 12 basis points of slippage and zero
commission, uses a fixed 12% initial invalidation stop, and leaves residual
cash as cash. Position sizing is equal-weighted; volatility scaling is not used.

## Predeclared controls

R6 is compared unchanged with:

- simple momentum inside the identical qualifying event universe;
- high-volume attention shocks without the near-high/follow-through gate;
- near-high five-session continuation without the volume shock;
- SPY and QQQ simple total returns; and
- deterministic random ranks inside the exact qualifying event universe and
  identical portfolio lifecycle.

The first stage uses 100 matched placebos to reject weak models efficiently. A
survivor must still pass at least 1,000 matched placebos before promotion.

## Gates

- Positive after-slippage return, expectancy, and profit factor above one in
  development, validation, and historical audit.
- Positive simple return differences versus both SPY and QQQ, positive alpha
  in at least half of windows, at least 70% average active-stock exposure,
  adequate closed trades, and maximum drawdown no worse than 25% in each phase.
- Superiority to every predeclared control in development and historical audit.
- Newey-West five-lag t-statistic above 3.0 versus both SPY and QQQ across
  validation and historical-audit windows.
- Empirical placebo p-value below 5% after Bonferroni adjustment for R1 through
  R6.
- Complete point-in-time membership and corrected adjusted-price integrity.

Failure retires R6 unchanged. Passing this historical screen only permits the
strict placebo stage and prospective paper tracking; it does not authorize live
capital.
