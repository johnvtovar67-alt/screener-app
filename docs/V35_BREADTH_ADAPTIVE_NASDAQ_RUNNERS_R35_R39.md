# R35-R39: Breadth-Adaptive Nasdaq Runner Research

Frozen: 2026-09-04

R30-R34 showed that R26's apparent benchmark advantage depended materially on
resetting the portfolio at six-month reporting boundaries. R35-R39 therefore
retain a continuous portfolio lifecycle and test a different economic
mechanism: faster leader refresh when contemporaneous Nasdaq momentum breadth
is weak, with slower runner retention when breadth is broad.

The frozen family includes one-to-three and two-to-four leader adaptation, a
severe-narrowness weekly refresh, a fixed two-leader cadence isolate, and a
volatility-budgeted dual-speed three-leader variant. Matched simple-momentum
and deterministic-random controls use the same lifecycle.

All decisions use information available at the decision date. Orders fill at
the next session open with whole shares, zero commission, and 12 basis points
of slippage per side. Uninvested capital remains cash. SPY and QQQ are
comparators only and never fill residual cash.

The 25% drawdown ceiling, positive after-cost return and expectancy, profit
factor above one, superiority to both SPY and QQQ, minimum closed trades,
exposure, fold stability, exact data-integrity requirements, strict 1,000-seed
maximum-statistic placebo, and at least 60 genuinely new forward sessions all
remain mandatory. Inspected history can reject this family but cannot promote
it into live authority.
