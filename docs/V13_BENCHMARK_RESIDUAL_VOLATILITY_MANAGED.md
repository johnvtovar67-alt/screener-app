# V13 / Research R3 benchmark-residual leadership contract

## Freeze and authority

This research-only thesis was frozen on 2026-09-03 after V12 and point-in-time
research generations R1 and R2 failed. It may not change production
recommendations. The live policy remains V11 unless every historical,
statistical, strict-placebo, prospective and independent-replication gate passes.

Every session through 2026-09-01 was already observable. Historical results can
falsify R3 but cannot, by themselves, establish future alpha.

## Hypothesis

Raw momentum can mostly reflect unstable market and technology-beta exposure.
R3 instead ranks liquid point-in-time S&P 500 members by persistent excess
return versus both SPY and QQQ, retains sector-relative leadership, and reduces
exposure to high-volatility and deep-drawdown names. Risk is targeted rather
than held constant across stocks.

The single primary rank is frozen as:

- 35% 120-session excess return versus SPY and QQQ;
- 25% 60-session excess return versus SPY and QQQ;
- 20% global and sector-aware intermediate momentum percentile;
- 10% low realized volatility;
- 7% 120-session drawdown resilience; and
- 3% controlled five-session pullback.

The portfolio holds twelve approximately 8.25% positions, rebalances every ten
sessions, retains names through rank 18, requires two qualified sessions, caps
a sector at four positions/34%, fills at the next open, charges 12 basis points
of slippage, enforces the 3% next-open gap ceiling, and leaves residual cash as
cash. It targets 18% stock volatility, rejects names above 55% trailing
60-session volatility, and uses the existing 18% initial invalidation stop.

## Predeclared controls

R3 is compared unchanged with:

- simple momentum under the identical lifecycle;
- the identical benchmark-residual rank without volatility management;
- a dual-benchmark relative-return-only ablation;
- SPY and QQQ simple total returns; and
- deterministic matched-lifecycle random ranks.

The first stage uses 100 placebos solely to reject weak models quickly. A model
that survives must run at least 1,000 matched placebos before any promotion.

## Statistical gates

- Positive return, expectancy, profit factor and adequate trade count in every
  historical phase.
- Beat both SPY and QQQ in the required share of windows.
- Beat every predeclared control in development and historical audit.
- Newey-West five-lag t-statistic above 3.0 versus both SPY and QQQ across the
  validation and historical-audit windows.
- Empirical placebo p-value below 5% after Bonferroni adjustment for R1, R2 and
  R3.
- Corrected point-in-time membership and adjusted-price integrity must pass.

Failure retires R3 without retuning it on these dates.
