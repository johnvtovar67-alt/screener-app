# V19 / R9 conviction sizing and replacement-stop batch

R8 identified one repeatable mechanism worth isolating: 120-session
dual-benchmark residual leadership with drawdown resilience. Its six-name
monthly portfolio returned 76.94% in development and beat SPY by 28.95%, but
missed QQQ by 12.47%. It beat both benchmarks in validation, then failed the
25% drawdown gate with a 34.55% decline.

R9 leaves that signal unchanged and tests portfolio construction only. One
batch contains six frozen rank-allocation schedules—equal, moderately
top-heavy and strongly top-heavy versions of four- and six-name portfolios—
crossed with fixed 10%, 14% and 18% replacement stops. All variants rebalance
every 20 sessions, fill at the next open, pay 12 basis points of slippage and
leave residual cash as cash. No benchmark sleeve, leverage, volatility
scaling, fundamental data or audit selection is permitted.

All 18 candidates share development restores. Four reach validation and only a
candidate passing every development and validation gate may reach audit. Audit
cannot select or retune. The separate 1,000-placebo stage remains deferred
unless the frozen audit candidate beats SPY and QQQ, stays within the exposure
and drawdown limits, and records audit-only Newey-West t-statistics above 3.0
against both indices.

Every stored date was already observable, so R9 can falsify portfolio
construction but cannot by itself prove future alpha. V11 remains live unless
the strict placebo, 60-new-session and independent-replication gates also pass.
