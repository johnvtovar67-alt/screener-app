# C1 automatic provider-revision reconciliation — September 22, 2026

Authorization: the owner directed C1 to accept verified revisions from its sole market-data provider through an append-only automatic reconciliation process rather than requiring another date-specific code patch. This repair does not change C1 selection, ranking, sizing, entry, exit, stop, cash, concentration or user-interface policy.

## Behavior

When FMP revises the immediately preceding adjusted daily history, C1 still preserves the originally accepted session. A revision can advance automatically only when every changed adjusted OHLCV bar appears identically in the current immutable observation and at least one earlier immutable observation. Confirmation is evaluated per changed symbol because FMP can finalize unrelated bars at different times; one still-changing symbol does not invalidate independently repeated evidence for another. The observations must use the FMP `historical-price-eod/dividend-adjusted` endpoint, retain the provider and twice-checked current membership, contain unique prior-date evidence rows and valid adjusted OHLCV bars. During a scheduled membership transition, constituents absent from the new membership retain their already accepted prior-session bars; only symbols common to the old session and new evidence are eligible for correction. The complete forward replay must still be identical. Provider fields that C1 does not consume do not affect confirmation.

C1 replays the next session from both the original and revised prior bars. Ledger, pending sleeves, paper execution, execution status and summary must match exactly. Any conflicting or incomplete provider evidence, invalid bar, benchmark/membership gap, blocked execution or economic difference retains the existing stop. No tolerance, inferred split, fabricated bar or alternate provider is introduced.

On success, C1 stores the original model result and appends a `c1-provider-revision-reconciliation-v1` receipt containing the provider, endpoint, normalized evidence hash, per-symbol confirmation hash and count, archived observation hashes, field-level differences, corrected comparison bars and economic-comparison hash. Previously accepted sessions, captures, fills, holdings, cash, stops and capital history are not rewritten.

Private accounts are checked independently. A repeat-verified revision to a symbol present in adopted holdings or recorded fills is appended to the opening receipt while the price that governed the recorded account remains unchanged. This prevents a later vendor finalization from rewriting actual ownership, fills or adoption economics. Revisions to unexposed symbols and benchmarks are replayed under both bars; continuation requires identical ownership, cash, actionable orders, entry reviews and pause decisions. Mark-only high-water changes do not disable the account when they leave those decisions unchanged. Unstable evidence or a changed stop, order, blocked order, entry decision, pause state, ownership or cash balance retains the existing stop.

## Verification

Focused regression coverage requires independent confirmation for every revised symbol, permits different observations to confirm different symbols, ignores unused provider fields, rejects missing or conflicting confirmations, preserves the accepted bar, rejects model-economic changes and rejects private-account plan changes. It also covers the September 22 defect in which the opening review rejected every revision to any current or former holding before performing its existing parity replay. The repaired review accepts repeat-verified held-symbol corrections as preserved-recorded-basis receipts, continues unrelated opening checks, accepts benchmark corrections only after actionable parity, and still rejects unstable evidence or action-changing corrections. A bounded, non-sensitive failure stage and unconfirmed-symbol count are logged for future diagnosis. The prior reviewed September 17 recovery remains supported for its already-recorded evidence.
