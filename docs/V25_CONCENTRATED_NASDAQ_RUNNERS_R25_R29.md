# R25-R29: Concentrated Nasdaq Runner Research

Frozen: 2026-09-04

R21 beat SPY with positive expectancy and a profit factor above three but
missed QQQ. R25-R29 test the bounded economic implication: a cap-weighted,
concentrated technology benchmark may require comparable concentration and
wider runner-retention buffers from an active momentum portfolio.

The batch contains one-, two-, and three-leader variants, a three-leader wide
rank buffer, a responsive dual-speed variant, an anchored two-leader variant,
and matched simple-momentum and random controls.

All candidates use the verified point-in-time Nasdaq membership dataset,
next-session-open execution, whole shares, zero commission, 12 basis points of
slippage per side, cash residuals, and unchanged SPY/QQQ comparisons.

All stored history is development-only. It may reject a hypothesis but cannot
validate or promote it. Any survivor must remain unchanged through the strict
1,000-seed maximum-statistic placebo and at least 60 genuinely new sessions.
