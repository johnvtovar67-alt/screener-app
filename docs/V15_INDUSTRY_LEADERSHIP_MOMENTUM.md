# V15 / R5 industry-leadership momentum

Frozen 2026-09-03 after V14/R4 failed. This is a distinct sector-first thesis,
not a retuning or promotion of a known R3 ablation. It is research-only.

## Thesis

Industry return persistence may explain more of stock momentum than isolated
stock ranks. The model first measures each contemporaneous sector's median
120-session excess return versus SPY and QQQ, then selects continuous stock
leaders within the strongest sectors.

The frozen rank is 45% sector trend, 25% stock dual-benchmark residual return,
15% within-sector momentum percentile, 10% continuous-information percentile,
and 5% low volatility. It holds 12 equal-weight stocks, permits five positions
or 43% per leading sector, rebalances every 20 sessions, requires 40 sessions
before rank exits, and uses 18% initial stops. It has no volatility-target or
benchmark-completion sleeve: selected stocks alone may invest up to 99%.

## Falsification contract

- one primary candidate; no parameter search;
- stock-residual-only, industry-only, and matched-random controls;
- 100 matched placebos for the bounded screen;
- family-wise placebo probability below 5%, counting R1 through R5;
- Newey-West five-lag t-statistic above 3 versus both SPY and QQQ;
- unchanged return, drawdown, trade-count, exposure and stability gates;
- any survivor still needs 1,000 placebos, 60 genuinely new sessions, and
  independent cross-universe replication before promotion.
