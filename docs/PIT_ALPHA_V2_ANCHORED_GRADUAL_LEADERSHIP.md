## Economic hypothesis

Among liquid S&P 500 members in an established uptrend, stocks trading close to
a recently established trailing 252-session high and reaching that level through
many small, same-direction advances should continue outperforming stocks whose
past return came from a few discontinuous jumps. This combines two distinct
underreaction mechanisms:

1. anchoring to a widely observed prior high; and
2. limited attention to information arriving gradually across many sessions.

The thesis deliberately separates intermediate performance from the latest
month. It does not interpret the largest recent price increase as the strongest
evidence.

## Fixed rank and lifecycle

The one authoritative candidate is `anchored-gradual-leadership-monthly-10`.
Its rank is frozen as:

- 28% trailing-high proximity;
- 10% recency of the trailing high;
- 22% continuity of the 120-session path;
- 15% intermediate-horizon leadership excluding the latest 20 sessions;
- 14% relative strength against SPY and QQQ;
- 6% drawdown resilience;
- 3% absence of a one-session volume shock; and
- 2% liquidity.

It holds ten approximately 9.5% positions, rebalances every 20 sessions, retains
names through rank 15 for turnover control, requires two consecutive qualified
sessions, caps a sector at four positions/40%, fills at the next open, applies
12 basis points of slippage, and leaves residual cash as cash. The generic
short-run chase veto is off because proximity to a prior high is part of the
hypothesis; a 3% next-open gap ceiling, continuity rank, volume-shock term and
18% initial invalidation stop remain.

## Evidence rules

Only values available at each historical close may enter the rank. Historical
S&P membership is applied session by session; orders fill at the following
session's open. Revision-unsafe fundamentals and incomplete historical news are
excluded. Exposure-matched alpha may be reported only as a diagnostic and may
never replace the simple SPY and QQQ comparisons.

Failure of any return, expectancy, profit-factor, drawdown, fold-stability,
exposure, data-integrity, benchmark, ablation, or placebo screen retires V2.
Failed results cannot be retuned on the same dates or enter live authority.
