# One current brokerage cash balance

The owner reports that the allocation-correction review displays a cash total
far above the actual brokerage balance. They explicitly require one current
cash balance, supplied broker activity and confirmed current cash. Inspection
shows the final purchase cap already consumes brokerage cash, but allocation
sizing, a sector ceiling and risk references consume a separate historical
residual. Core Add/Update changes only a portfolio snapshot, so the cash effects
of Core trades and transfers never entered that residual. Broker activity also
identifies missing older cash flows and recorded trade-net discrepancies.

Preserve the original adoption, daily records, captured opening books, fills,
in-kind receipt and stop facts. Validate them through the original strict
replay. Derive a separate financial view using an owner-reviewed, hash-bound
statement journal. Statement net amounts are cash evidence; rounded displayed
prices do not establish unrounded execution prices. Core trades and transfers
are external capital, while interest, fees and trade cash adjustments are P&L.
Do not double-debit an in-kind adoption or credit an existing sale twice.

Use brokerage cash as the single displayed/current spendable balance. Keep
original accounting only as audit evidence, never another wallet. All current
sizing, exposure and operational risk must consume the same corrected financial
view. Capital flows use proportional marked allocation equity, preserving its
relative allocation and drawdown percentage; they do not manufacture trading
losses or establish fresh high-water marks. Keep the original recorded peaks,
curves, triggers and loss history available separately. Existing stop levels,
stock cooldown clocks, four-name capacity, selection and 12% protection remain.
Completed stock-loss classification uses the reviewed statement net amounts,
including fees, without rewriting the original fills or purchase basis. Core
capital movements and cash interest do not become losses in a stock's clock.

Corrections retain stable logical exit-episode identities independent of their
revised financial-proof digest, preventing repeated resets after a correction.
Any reference reset still requires a complete recorded liquidation and the
next eligible exchange opening. Never infer a trade from a share-count or
average-cost snapshot edit. Future cash movements require explicit dated facts
and confirmation; uncertain financial writes are not automatically retried.
After a later Core edit, current purchase review requires a newer brokerage
cash observation. A balance change that leaves a material unexplained accounting
difference also requires dated cash history. Previously issued, actually filled
trades retain their historical recording path. Ephemeral review flags are never
saved as financial records.

Financial plan lineage retains the original approved journal boundary and its
confirmation instant. A new plan on that same session must be observed after
confirmation and match the complete trusted opening books. Current-session
income or minor balance corrections apply once whether or not a trade exists;
accepted close processing must not apply the same fills or cash postings twice.
Later statement reviews validate each captured financial plan against its own
original approved journal, then apply newly documented flows to the derived
books. Verified capital changes scale the corresponding risk reference rather
than manufacture a loss episode. Completed sales remain immutable facts when
the refreshed model queue changes; only fresh, fully gated quantities can
become new instructions. A recorded loss exit still prevents that allocation's
new purchases until the completed-exit and next-opening requirements pass.

The owner explicitly reaffirms keeping allocation-based partial exits while
repairing repeated sale notices. Available evidence does not establish superior
after-cost performance for broader whole-stock or account-wide liquidations;
this release makes neither policy change nor investment-performance claim.
Keep statement journals and financial replay out of the browser dependency
graph. The browser and server share the unchanged pure opening-receipt
validator; no Node compatibility shim or build/config exception is used.

All private evidence and owner figures stay outside version control. Public
regressions use fictional data. Preserve old source/economic fixtures and pins,
the original canonical simulator and existing guard/CI/build implementations.
Update only scoped application freeze entries and exact new source inverses.
Require actual parser/domain/API/form paths, full local build/regression/audit,
all four exact-head CI jobs, matching preview/merged tree/production code, and
the owner's actual review-confirm-reload path. No account or brokerage write
is authorized merely by deployment or by passing tests.
